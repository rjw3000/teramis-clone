Precision CUI Discovery. Defensible CUI Scoping.

Teramis helps Defense Industrial Base organizations find, validate, report, and monitor Controlled Unclassified Information across approved environments.

Our platform gives security, compliance, IT, legal, executive, and advisory teams a clearer view of where CUI actually exists so scope can be built on evidence instead of assumptions.

Teramis Exists to Make CUI Scope Defensible

Every CUI safeguarding decision depends on knowing where Controlled Unclassified Information lives. But in real environments, CUI can spread across Microsoft 365, shared drives, endpoints, engineering repositories, email attachments, project folders, scanned documents, and legacy storage.

Teramis helps organizations replace guesswork with discovery evidence.

We identify potential CUI, validate findings, report what matters, and monitor changes over time so teams can make better decisions about scope, boundaries, risk, and readiness.

You Cannot Scope What You Cannot See

Before an organization can define a boundary, plan an enclave, prepare for assessment, or support a partner-led compliance effort, it needs to understand its actual data footprint.

Documentation can describe the intended environment. Teramis helps show what is actually present.

That evidence can support conversations about:

  • CUI scope
  • CUI boundaries
  • Data locations
  • Repository and file-level findings
  • Boundary exceptions
  • Migration planning
  • Spillage monitoring
  • Assessment-preparation discussions

Teramis helps teams start with the data picture before committing to larger decisions.

What We Believe

CUI scope should not be built only from assumptions, interviews, or inherited documentation. It should be supported by findings that teams can review and act on.

A documented boundary is only useful if it matches the environment. Teramis helps organizations compare approved CUI locations with what discovery actually shows.

CUI environments change. New files appear, teams shift, projects expand, and data can move outside expected locations. Teramis helps organizations monitor those changes over time.

Teramis provides discovery evidence. Your team, your advisors, and your approved service providers decide what actions to take.

What We Do

Teramis helps organizations:

  • Discover potential CUI across approved data sources
  • Validate findings through review and sampling workflows
  • Identify where CUI appears by file, repository, and location
  • Surface potential CUI outside expected or documented boundaries
  • Support CUI scoping and boundary-validation discussions
  • Monitor changes between scans
  • Provide evidence that teams can use for planning and decision-making

Teramis identifies, validates, reports, and monitors Controlled Unclassified Information.

Through Remedius, Teramis moves approved CUI to authorized locations, verifies every transfer, removes or tombstones unauthorized copies, and documents what changed. Your team approves every action. Teramis does not certify an organization or guarantee a compliance, assessment, or audit outcome.

Teramis provides the evidence and carries out approved remediation. Your team decides what happens.

Purpose-built CUI discovery for organizations that need scoping decisions based on evidence, not assumptions.

Who We Help

Built for Organizations That Need Defensible CUI Visibility

Teramis supports teams across the Defense Industrial Base and related environments, including:

  • Small and mid-sized defense contractors building their NIST SP 800-171 program
  • Enterprise organizations managing complex and distributed data environments
