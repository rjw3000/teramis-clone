Built to Make CUI Discovery More Defensible

Teramis helps defense contractors, enterprise teams, government agencies, and compliance partners discover CUI, ITAR, and other covered defense information, validate documented boundaries, remediate what doesn't belong, and monitor what changes over time. Every CUI safeguarding decision depends on knowing where that data actually lives. In real environments, it can appear across Microsoft 365, shared drives, endpoints, engineering repositories, email attachments, project folders, scanned documents, archives, and legacy storage. Teramis gives teams a clearer evidence base for scoping, boundary validation, remediation, monitoring, self-assessment, and partner-led compliance work.
