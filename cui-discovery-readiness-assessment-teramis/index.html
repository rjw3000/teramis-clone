Most CUI boundaries are drawn before teams know where their CUI actually lives.

Many organizations start with interviews, asset inventories, spreadsheets, and assumptions. That approach can lead to over-scoped environments, under-scoped risk, wasted licensing, and assessment rework.

A documented CUI boundary is only defensible if it matches what actually exists across the data environment.

Who Should Request This

Is our CUI scope built on assumptions?

Validate scope with data, not interviews and spreadsheets.

Are we building or validating an enclave?

Confirm what data needs to be in, and what can stay out.

Are we worried about CUI spillage?

Identify CUI outside the boundary before it becomes a problem.

Are generic tools creating too much noise?

Get focused results that separate CUI from benign data.
