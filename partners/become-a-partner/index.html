FIND THE RIGHT ENGAGEMENT MODEL

Different Partners Need Different Ways to Work Together

Not every partner relationship looks the same. Teramis can support organizations that want to introduce opportunities, incorporate CUI discovery into existing engagements, or build Teramis-enabled services around client needs.

Referral Partners

Introduce clients that need better visibility into where CUI exists and where documented scope may not match the actual environment.

Advisory and Compliance Partners

Use Teramis findings to support scoping, boundary validation, readiness, and ongoing compliance engagements.

Managed Service Providers

Add discovery and monitoring capabilities to infrastructure, security, enclave, and managed compliance services.

Technology and Integration Partners

Connect Teramis findings with the tools, workflows, and platforms already supporting the client environment.
