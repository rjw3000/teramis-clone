Add CUI Discovery to What Your Team Already Delivers

The Teramis partner program is built for organizations that already advise, manage, or secure clients that handle CUI, and that need technical evidence of where Controlled Unclassified Information actually lives.

This page covers how the program works in practice, the engagement paths available, what each side is responsible for, and what you can expect once you join.

THE PROBLEM EVERY ENGAGEMENT HITS

Your client cannot answer the first question

Client interviews, spreadsheets, asset inventories, and diagrams describe an intended environment. They do not show where CUI was actually found.

That gap slows scoping conversations, weakens boundary recommendations, and creates late-stage surprises that erode client confidence in the engagement.

Teramis gives your team discovery findings from the client’s approved data sources, so recommendations rest on evidence rather than recall.

TWO WAYS TO WORK WITH TERAMIS

Choose the path that matches how you sell and deliver

Confirm both path names, eligibility, and all commercial terms before publishing.

Referral and co-sell

For partners who introduce opportunities and stay involved in the client relationship without delivering the discovery work themselves.

Typical fit: Advisory firms and consultancies that want to strengthen client recommendations without adding a delivery motion..

Reseller and service delivery

For partners who build Teramis into their own service offering and deliver discovery, validation, and monitoring as part of client engagements.

Typical fit: MSPs, MSSPs, and providers running recurring compliance or managed security services who want a repeatable CUI offering.

Both paths use the same product and the same evidence. They differ in who delivers the work and who owns the client commercial relationship.

WHERE IT FITS IN YOUR SERVICES

Attach discovery to engagements you already run

CUI scoping

Help clients understand where CUI exists before defining systems, repositories, users, and assets within scope.

CUI boundary validation

Compare a client’s documented boundary with actual findings and identify exceptions that require review.

Spillage monitoring

Use recurring scans to identify new findings, changes between scans, and CUI appearing outside approved locations.

CUI remediation

Support clients in acting on validated findings with controlled movement and documentation.

Depends on the pending guardrail decision. Confirm before publishing.

Post-incident CUI review

Support factual discovery in affected systems or repositories after a suspected incident.

Teramis does not make legal, reporting, or notification determinations.

CLEAR LINES FROM THE START

Know what Teramis provides and what stays with you

Teramis provides

  • The discovery, validation, and monitoring capability
  • Findings from the client’s approved data sources
  • Documentation and evidence output
  • Partner enablement materials
  • Product support

Partners provide

  • The client relationship and engagement context
  • Interpretation of findings against the client’s compliance objectives
  • Recommendations and next steps
  • Any advisory, assessment-preparation, or remediation services you deliver
  • Final guidance to the client

Teramis supplies the evidence layer. Partners own the client outcome.
