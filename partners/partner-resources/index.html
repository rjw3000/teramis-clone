Equip Your Team to Position and Deliver CUI Discovery

Successful partner engagements require more than product access. Your team needs clear positioning, effective discovery questions, client-ready materials, and practical guidance for incorporating Teramis into the services you already provide.

Teramis partner resources help your team explain the value of CUI discovery, support stronger client conversations, and move opportunities toward a well-defined next step.

FROM FIRST CONVERSATION TO ONGOING DELIVERY

Give Your Team the Right Material at Every Stage

Discuss enablement with Teramis based on your partner type, delivery model, and level of involvement in the client engagement.

01 / POSITION AND SELL

Start Stronger Client Conversations

Help business development and advisory teams introduce CUI discovery clearly and connect it to the client’s actual concerns.

Ask about:

  • Positioning and messaging guidance
  • Discovery-call questions
  • One-pagers and introduction materials
  • Objection-handling guidance

02 / DEMONSTRATE AND EDUCATE

Make Discovery Easier to Understand

Give prospective clients a clearer understanding of what CUI discovery examines, produces, and supports.

  • Product walkthrough materials
  • Demo guidance
  • Client education content
  • Support for qualified opportunities

03 / DELIVER AND EXPAND

Prepare Your Delivery Team

Help delivery teams incorporate discovery, validation, and monitoring into existing client services.

  • Partner onboarding
  • Engagement-planning guidance
  • Team enablement
  • Recurring-monitoring conversations

Resource availability depends on partner type, engagement path, and current program participation. Contact Teramis to confirm the materials available to your team.

DIFFERENT PARTNERS NEED DIFFERENT SUPPORT

Resources Built Around How You Serve Clients

An MSP building a recurring monitoring service needs different enablement than an advisor supporting a CUI scoping engagement. Teramis aligns resources to the role your organization plays.

MSPs and MSSPs

Build discovery, boundary validation, and recurring monitoring into managed security, compliance, IT, and vCISO services.

Compliance Advisors and Consultants

Use technical CUI findings to strengthen scoping, readiness, and boundary-validation conversations.

GRC and Enclave Providers

Connect documentation, enclave design, and migration planning to the client’s actual CUI footprint.

Technology Partners

Help mutual customers identify CUI before applying governance, labeling, access, or enforcement policies.
