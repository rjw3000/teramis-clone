Four Kinds of Partners, One Missing Answer

Advisors, managed service providers, compliance platforms, and technology vendors all run into the same question when clients handle CUI: where is it, and how do we know?

Teramis supplies that answer as discovery evidence your team can use inside the services you already deliver.

MSPs and MSSPs

Build a Repeatable CUI Discovery Offering

Add CUI discovery and recurring monitoring to managed security, compliance, IT, and vCISO services.

  • Build a repeatable CUI discovery offering rather than a one-off project
  • Support ongoing visibility after initial scoping is complete
  • Give clients clear findings and defined next steps
  • Attach recurring monitoring to existing managed service agreements

Compliance Advisors and Consultants

Support Scope Recommendations With Technical Findings

GRC and Enclave Providers

Connect Documentation and Enclaves to the Actual CUI Footprint

Tie SSP work, evidence binders, readiness planning, and enclave design to where CUI was actually found.

  • Strengthen documentation with discovery-backed evidence
  • Identify gaps between written scope and real data location
  • Confirm what needs to move before an enclave is sized or built
  • Support more defensible compliance discussions

Technology Partners

Complement Your Platform With CUI Evidence

Add discovery findings to secure collaboration, governance, labeling, and reporting solutions.

  • Help customers identify CUI before applying policy
  • Strengthen solution value with verified findings
  • Explore joint opportunities where there is a strong fit
