Find the CUI You Actually Have

Teramis discovers CUI, ITAR, and other covered defense information, wherever it lives.

Controlled Unclassified Information can exist far beyond the systems and folders where teams expect to find it.

Teramis examines supported data sources to identify where CUI, ITAR, and other covered defense information exists, surface unexpected locations, and provide a stronger technical foundation for scoping, boundary, and remediation decisions.

Purpose-built CUI discovery with scanning and analysis performed within the customer environment.

Why CUI Discovery Comes First

Your CUI Boundary Is Only as Accurate as the Data Behind It

Asset inventories, architecture diagrams, policies, and employee interviews can describe where CUI is supposed to be. They do not necessarily show where it actually exists.

Files get copied, forwarded, downloaded, archived, renamed, scanned, and stored in unexpected places. When those locations are missed, the documented boundary can drift from the actual data environment.

Over-Scoped

Protecting More Than the Evidence Requires

Systems that do not need to be included may add unnecessary licensing, security, assessment, and support requirements.

Under-Scoped

CUI Exists Outside the Documented Boundary

CUI may remain outside the documented boundary, creating gaps that require investigation and correction.

Evidence before assumptions.

WHERE IS THE CUI?

CUI Discovery Starts With the Data Environment

Controlled Unclassified Information does not always live in the folder someone designated for it. It can appear in technical documents, engineering files, PDFs, email attachments, images, scans, compressed archives, project folders, and repositories that have grown over years.

Teramis is built for the messy reality of defense data. It helps organizations examine approved environments and supported file types so CUI discovery is not limited to obvious labels, expected folders, or simple keyword searches.

Find the data first. Then draw the boundary.
