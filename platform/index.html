Find the CUI. Prove the Boundary. Remediate. Monitor What Changes.

Teramis is a CUI discovery platform that helps Defense Industrial Base organizations find Controlled Unclassified Information, validate their documented boundary, and monitor for changes over time.

See what exists wherever it lives and give your compliance, security, and advisory teams evidence they can act on.

Purpose-built CUI discovery with scanning and analysis performed within the customer environment.

WHY DISCOVERY COMES FIRST

CUI Scoping Starts With Knowing Where the Data Is

Policies, interviews, asset inventories, and diagrams describe where CUI is expected to reside. They do not always show where it exists today.

CUI can appear in email attachments, shared drives, project folders, endpoints, engineering repositories, scanned documents, archives, and other locations that are difficult to assess through assumptions alone.

Teramis provides the discovery and validation layer needed to compare the documented boundary with the data environment you actually have.

CUI scoping becomes stronger when boundary decisions are supported by evidence.

You cannot defend what you cannot prove.

Trusted by leading companies

HOW TERAMIS WORKS

Find the CUI. Prove the Boundary. Monitor What Changes.

Teramis examines supported data sources to identify potential Controlled Unclassified Information, validate findings, document the CUI footprint, and monitor for changes over time.

This gives compliance, security, IT, and advisory teams evidence to support CUI scoping and boundary decisions.

Define Scope

Choose the systems, repositories, business areas, and data sources Teramis is authorized to examine. Your team controls what is included in the discovery effort.

Scan for CUI Across the Environment

Teramis examines supported repositories and file types for content that may contain CUI, including Microsoft 365, file systems, endpoints, email, engineering repositories, archives, and other supported sources.

Review and Validate Findings

Review where potential CUI was found, which files and repositories require attention, and which findings may fall outside the documented boundary.

Validation helps distinguish relevant findings from unrelated content and supports a more defensible CUI inventory.

Remediate

Take controlled action on validated CUI findings by moving data to authorized locations, removing unauthorized copies, and documenting what happened.

Remedius, a remediation capability within Teramis, helps organizations:

  • Move identified CUI from file systems, SharePoint, and OneDrive to authorized destinations
  • Preserve source folder structures during migration
  • Verify successful transfers using hash comparison before source files are removed
  • Delete or replace unauthorized source files with tombstones
  • Manage file conflicts and unsuccessful transfers
  • Generate transfer manifests and logs documenting remediation Explore CUI Remediation

Monitor What Changes

Run recurring scans to identify new CUI, potential spillage, and changes to the data environment.

Teramis helps organizations monitor:

  • New CUI
  • CUI outside approved locations
  • Changes between scans
  • Boundary drift
  • Recurring spillage risk
  • Findings requiring remediation
