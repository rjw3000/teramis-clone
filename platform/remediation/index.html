Remediate Validated CUI with Evidence

Finding CUI is only the first step. Once Controlled Unclassified Information is discovered and validated, teams need a controlled way to move it to authorized locations, remove unauthorized copies, and document what happened.

Remedius, a remediation capability within Teramis, helps organizations act on validated CUI findings with controlled movement, transfer verification, tombstones, manifests, and logs.

Purpose-built CUI remediation with controlled movement, transfer verification, tombstones, manifests, and logs performed within the customer environment. Teramis helps organizations find, prove, remediate, and monitor CUI wherever it lives.

FROM FINDINGS TO ACTION : WHERE IS THE CUI?

CUI Remediation Should Not Start With Guesswork

When CUI appears outside approved locations, teams need more than a list of possible matches. They need to know what was found, where it lives, whether it belongs there, and what action should happen next.

Without validated evidence, remediation can create new problems:

  • Teams may act on false positives
  • CUI may remain outside the approved boundary
  • Files may be moved without enough documentation
  • Unauthorized copies may remain behind
  • Remediation activity may not produce the evidence your self-assessment and documentation need
  • Leadership may not know whether the boundary actually improved

Teramis helps reduce that uncertainty by connecting validated findings to controlled remediation activity and clear documentation.

REMEDIUS WITHIN THE TERAMIS PLATFORM

Move Validated CUI Into Authorized Locations

Remedius helps organizations take controlled action on validated CUI findings by moving data to authorized locations, removing unauthorized copies, and documenting remediation activity.

Remedius helps organizations:

  • Move identified CUI from file systems, SharePoint, and OneDrive to authorized destinations
  • Preserve source folder structures during migration
  • Verify successful transfers using hash comparison before source files are removed
  • Delete or replace unauthorized source files with tombstones
  • Manage file conflicts and unsuccessful transfers
  • Generate transfer manifests and logs documenting remediation activity

CONTROLLED CUI ACTION

Know What Changed, Where It Went, and What Was Documented

Teramis helps answer practical remediation questions such as:

  • Which validated CUI findings require action?
  • Where is the source file located?
  • Where should the CUI be moved?
  • Was the transfer successful?
  • Was the source file removed or replaced with a tombstone?
  • Were file conflicts or failed transfers captured?
  • What manifest or log documents the remediation activity?
  • What needs to be monitored again after remediation?

This gives security, compliance, IT, and partner teams a clearer record of what was discovered, remediated, and reviewed.
