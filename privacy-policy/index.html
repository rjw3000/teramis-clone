Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026Last updated: September 21, 2026

Teramis, Inc. ("Teramis," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information collected through our website, including teramis.us and related Teramis-hosted pages (collectively, the "Site").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information when you visit the Site, submit a form, request information, register for an event, download a resource, or otherwise communicate with us.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We may collect information you choose to provide, including:

  • Name
  • Business email address
  • Phone number
  • Company name
  • Job title
  • Organization type
  • Information included in form submissions, meeting requests, or other communications

Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Site, we and our service providers may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and device information
  • Operating system
  • Pages viewed and links selected
  • Date, time, and duration of visits
  • Referring website or URL
  • General location derived from an IP address
  • Cookie and similar-technology identifiers
  • Information about interactions with forms, emails, advertisements, and Site features

How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries, demo requests, and other communications
  • Provide requested resources, events, or information
  • Manage business relationships and communications
  • Evaluate and improve the Site, content, products, and services
  • Communicate about Teramis offerings, events, and resources, where permitted by law
  • Measure Site usage and the effectiveness of our communications and advertising
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the Site
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, or other unauthorized activity
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in an email or contacting us using the information below.

Do Not Send Sensitive Information Through Website Forms

Do not submit Controlled Unclassified Information, credentials, passwords, file paths, incident evidence, export-controlled information, classified information, or other sensitive information through the Site or its forms.

Website forms are intended for general business inquiries only. Teramis will provide an appropriate method for sharing sensitive information if needed after initial contact.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Teramis and its service providers use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Site, remember visitor preferences, understand Site usage, improve visitor experience, and measure the effectiveness of communications and advertising.

These technologies may include:

  • Essential technologies needed to operate, secure, and maintain the Site
  • Functional technologies used to remember preferences and support Site features
  • Analytics technologies used to understand Site traffic and visitor interactions
  • Advertising technologies used to measure campaign performance and support relevant business advertising

Service providers used for these purposes may include HubSpot, Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and LinkedIn, depending on the technologies currently enabled on the Site.
