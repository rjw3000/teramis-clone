Give Your Scoping Recommendations Technical Evidence

Client interviews, spreadsheets, asset inventories, and diagrams describe the intended environment. They do not establish where CUI actually lives.

Teramis helps compliance advisors and consultants replace assumption-based scoping with discovery findings from wherever CUI actually lives in the client's environment.

The Advisory Challenge

Recommendations Are Only as Strong as the Evidence Behind Them

Relying on interviews and documentation alone can lead to over-scoping, under-scoping, and late-stage surprises that undermine confidence in the engagement.

Teramis gives compliance advisors and consultants discovery findings from wherever CUI actually lives in the client's environment, so scope recommendations rest on evidence instead of recall.

Trusted by leading companies

What Teramis Helps Advisors Do

Establish a Client's CUI Baseline

Identify where potential CUI exists in a client's environment before scope is finalized.

Strengthen Scoping Conversations

Bring discovery findings into client conversations instead of relying on interviews and assumptions alone.

Surface Unexpected Locations Early

Identify potential CUI outside the locations a client expects or has documented, before it becomes a late-stage surprise.

Support Ongoing Client Engagements

Use recurring scans to give clients continued visibility as their environment changes.

Where This Fits in an Advisory Engagement

Early Scoping Conversations

Bring discovery findings into the first conversation about a client's CUI boundary.

Validating an Existing Boundary

Test whether a client's documented boundary reflects what's actually in their environment.

Ongoing Advisory Relationships

Add recurring monitoring to engagements where clients need continued visibility, not just a one-time assessment.

EVIDENCE YOU CAN BRING TO CLIENTS

Findings That Support Defensible Recommendations

Teramis can provide:

  • CUI inventory information
  • File and repository details
  • Findings outside expected locations
  • Boundary exceptions for review
  • Validation and sampling output
  • Change reporting between scans
  • Monitoring history

This evidence can support client-facing scoping conversations, compliance decisions, and the documentation you help clients produce.

Works Alongside Your Advisory Practice

Teramis Supports Your Recommendations

Teramis gives compliance advisors discovery findings from a client's approved data sources. You bring the judgment, documentation, and client relationship needed to turn those findings into recommendations.

Teramis does not replace a GRC platform, your advisory practice, or a formal assessment.

What Teramis Does and Does Not Do

Teramis helps identify, validate, report on, and monitor Controlled Unclassified Information. Its findings can also support remediation planning and customer-authorized movement into approved locations.

Teramis does not make assessment or legal determinations or guarantee a compliance outcome. Advisors and their clients remain responsible for interpreting the evidence and deciding what action to take.
