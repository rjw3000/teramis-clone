Make Enterprise CUI Visibility Defensible

Large Microsoft 365 tenants, engineering repositories, acquired subsidiaries, legacy storage, and distributed teams make it difficult to know where Controlled Unclassified Information actually exists.

Teramis helps enterprise organizations discover CUI wherever it lives, validate whether the documented boundary matches reality, and monitor what changes over time.

Purpose-built CUI discovery with scanning and analysis performed within the customer environment.

The Enterprise Challenge

Scale Makes the CUI Boundary Harder to Trust

Enterprise data rarely stays in one place. CUI may exist across business units, project folders, collaboration platforms, endpoints, file shares, and systems inherited through acquisition or organizational change.

Documentation can describe the intended environment without showing the complete data footprint.

Teramis gives legal, security, IT, compliance, and executive teams a shared record of the findings that matter.
