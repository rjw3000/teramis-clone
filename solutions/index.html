Start With the CUI Decision You Need to Make

Different organizations face different CUI challenges. Teramis helps you identify where Controlled Unclassified Information exists, validate your documented boundary, and monitor what changes over time.

Use the evidence to make more informed decisions about CUI scoping, boundary validation, migration planning, spillage monitoring, incident review, and supply chain risk.

START WITH THE DATA

Every CUI Decision Begins With the Same Question

Where does your CUI actually live?

Policies, interviews, inventories, and diagrams describe an intended environment. Teramis helps you examine supported data sources and compare that intended state with the findings in your actual environment.

That evidence can support decisions about:

  • CMMC scope
  • System and data boundaries
  • Migration planning
  • CUI spillage monitoring
  • Post-incident review
  • Supply chain and M&A due diligence

The right solution starts with a CUI footprint that can be examined, explained, and defended.

SOLUTIONS BY DECISION

Choose the Problem You Are Solving

Teramis provides the CUI ground truth organizations need to support compliance decisions, self-attestation, and ongoing CUI governance.

CUI Scoping

Define the CUI Boundary With Evidence

Identify where CUI exists before deciding which systems, repositories, users, and assets belong inside the assessment boundary.

CUI Boundary Validation

Test Whether the Documented Boundary Matches Reality

Compare your documented boundary with actual findings and surface CUI discovered outside expected or approved locations.

CUI Remediation & Spillage Monitoring

Monitor for CUI Outside Approved Locations

Run recurring scans to identify new findings, changes between scans, and CUI appearing outside the locations your team expects.

Post-Incident CUI Review

Examine Affected Systems for Potential CUI

Use targeted discovery to help determine whether CUI may have existed in affected systems or repositories after a security incident.

Teramis supports factual discovery. It does not make legal, reporting, or notification determinations.

Migration Planning Support

Know What Requires Review Before a Move

Identify files and locations that administrators or approved service providers may need to review when planning authorized CUI migration activity.

Teramis reports findings, and Remedius carries out the actions your team approves.

Supply Chain and M&A Review

Test CUI Representations With Evidence

Examine whether representations about an organization’s CUI footprint are supported by findings from the actual environment.

Use cases include supplier reviews, acquisitions, integration planning, and data-separation planning.
