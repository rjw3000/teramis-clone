Migrate the CUI You Actually Have, Not the CUI You Think You Have

Enclave builds and tenant migrations are usually scoped from an inventory someone assembled by asking around. That inventory decides how large the destination needs to be, how long the project takes, and what it costs.

Teramis identifies and validates the actual CUI footprint before the move. After findings are confirmed, authorized personnel can remediate or migrate the CUI to a secure, approved location. Teramis can then verify that the source locations have been cleared.

A migration is only complete when the CUI has been moved to an approved location and the source has been verified.

You Can Only Migrate What You Know About

Migration projects are scoped from an assumed data set. When that assumption is wrong, the consequences show up late and cost real money:

  • The destination is sized for less data than actually needs to move
  • CUI is discovered mid-project, forcing rescoping and schedule changes
  • Licensing and infrastructure estimates are built on an incomplete picture
  • Data is copied rather than moved, leaving CUI in the original location
  • Nobody can confirm the source was cleared after cutover
  • The next discovery scan finds CUI still sitting in the environment you thought you left

The migration succeeds on paper while the boundary problem stays exactly where it was.

Establish What Actually Needs to Move

Teramis examines supported data sources to identify where CUI exists across the current environment, including locations the migration plan may not account for.

That gives the project team:

  • A file-level view of what is in scope for the move
  • Repository and location detail for planning sequence and effort
  • Findings in locations the inventory did not include
  • A basis for sizing the destination environment
  • Evidence to support licensing, infrastructure, and timeline estimates
  • A documented baseline to compare against after cutover

Size the destination from findings, not from a survey.

Move Data With a Record of What Happened

Remedius supports movement of identified CUI from file systems, SharePoint, and OneDrive into the destinations your organization authorizes, preserving source folder structure and verifying successful transfer using hash comparison before source files are removed.

File conflicts and unsuccessful transfers are reported rather than passed over, so nothing is assumed to have completed.

Authorized Destination

Movement is directed only to destinations approved by your organization.

Structure Preserved

Source folder structure can be maintained during supported movement workflows.

Transfer Verified

Hash comparison helps verify successful transfer before source removal.

Exceptions Reported

Conflicts and unsuccessful transfers remain visible for review.

What Teramis Does Not Do

  • Approve or design the destination environment
  • Decide which data must move
  • Perform the broader IT migration outside CUI scope
  • Provide licensing, infrastructure, or architecture recommendations
  • Guarantee a migration outcome or an assessment result
  • Make legal or contractual determinations

Projects That Depend on Knowing the Footprint First

Enclave Builds

Scope and size the enclave from the actual CUI footprint rather than an estimate.

Cloud and Tenant Migrations

Move CUI into an approved cloud environment and confirm the source is cleared.

Tenant Consolidation

Identify CUI across multiple tenants or domains before consolidating.
