CUI Discovery for Organizations That Need Defensible Scope

Whether you are building a NIST SP 800-171 program, supporting defense contractors, managing a complex enterprise environment, or reviewing sensitive information before it is shared, the first question is the same:

Where does Controlled Unclassified Information actually live?

Teramis helps organizations discover CUI wherever it lives, validate documented boundaries, and monitor what changes over time.

Purpose-built CUI discovery with scanning and analysis performed within the customer environment.

ONE PROBLEM ACROSS EVERY ENVIRONMENT

Different Organizations. The Same Core Problem.

Interviews, spreadsheets, asset inventories, and diagrams can describe where CUI is expected to reside. They do not always show where it exists today.

Teramis provides precision CUI discovery, validation, reporting, and ongoing monitoring so teams can make better decisions from evidence, not assumptions.

Trusted by leading companies

BUILT FOR THE DEFENSE INDUSTRIAL BASE

Find the Teramis Path for Your Organization

Teramis gives organizations and compliance partners technical CUI evidence they can use to make better decisions about scope, boundaries, monitoring, and next steps.

Small Business

Find the CUI your organization actually handles before finalizing scope, purchasing licenses, designing an enclave, or preparing for assessment. Teramis helps small businesses focus their next steps on the environment the evidence supports.

Compliance Advisors

Support CUI scoping recommendations with technical CUI evidence. Teramis helps advisors identify where client CUI actually exists, validate whether the documented boundary matches the real environment, and reduce reliance on interviews, spreadsheets, and assumptions.

Enterprise

Examine complex Microsoft 365 environments, distributed storage, engineering repositories, legacy systems, acquired environments, and large volumes of unstructured data. Teramis gives security, IT, legal, compliance, and leadership teams a clearer view of the actual CUI footprint.

Government Agencies

Improve visibility into CUI before sensitive information enters publication, sharing, transfer, or external-release workflows. Teramis provides technical findings that help authorized agency personnel make better-informed handling decisions.

Partners and Service Providers

MSPs, MSSPs, vCISOs, GRC providers, enclave partners, and other organizations serving the Defense Industrial Base can strengthen client recommendations with technical CUI findings that complement the services and platforms they already provide.
